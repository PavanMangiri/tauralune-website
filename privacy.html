2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • the website at https://tauralune.com and its pages;
  • emails you send to support@tauralune.com; and
  • Android apps published by Tauralune on Google Play, the Samsung Galaxy Store or other app stores (“our apps”).

At the time of this update, Tauralune has not yet published any apps. The app sections of this policy describe how our apps will handle data when they are released. Each app’s store listing will also show its own data disclosures (for example, the Data safety section on Google Play). If an app handles data in a way not described here, we will update this policy before that app is released.

3. Data from this website

The website is a static site. It has no user accounts, does not use cookies of its own, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from third parties.

  • Theme preference. If you switch between light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage on your own device. It is never sent to us.
  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Pages) or GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages). Like any web host, they process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies for this purpose. We do not use these logs to identify you.
  • Contact form. The form on our home page does not send data to any server. It only opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing reaches us unless you send that email.

4. Data you send us by email

When you email support@tauralune.com, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write or attach, such as the app version, phone model, Android version, screenshots or logs. Our email is hosted by Zoho Corporation (Zoho Mail, India data centre).

Please do not send passwords, OTPs, payment card details or government ID numbers by email. We will never ask for them.

5. Data in our Android apps

We design our apps to be local-first. This means:

  • Data you create in an app (notes, settings, lists, themes and similar) is stored on your device by default and is not uploaded to us.
  • Apps do not require an account unless a feature clearly needs one, and we will tell you before you create one.
  • Apps request only the Android permissions a feature needs, and explain why before asking.
  • Uninstalling an app removes the data it stored on your device, unless you chose to back it up or export it elsewhere (for example, Android’s own backup to your Google account).

Some apps may collect limited data to work reliably or to be offered free of charge. This can include:

Type of dataExampleWhy
Crash and diagnostic dataCrash logs, app version, device model, Android versionTo find and fix bugs
App usage dataWhich screens or features are usedTo improve the app (only if the app’s store listing says so)
Device and advertising identifiersAndroid advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IPTo show ads and limit ad fraud (only in apps that show ads)
Purchase informationOrder ID and purchase status from the app storeTo unlock paid features (we never receive your card details)

We do not sell personal data. We do not collect contacts, SMS, call logs, photos or precise location unless an app’s core feature requires it and you grant permission, and any such use will be shown in that app’s store listing.

6. Third-party services in apps

If an app uses any of the services below, its store listing and in-app privacy information will say so. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): advertising. May use the advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads. Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): advertising. Policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC): crash reports and app usage statistics.
  • Google Play Billing and Samsung In-App Purchase: payments, which are processed entirely by the store.

Your ad choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings (on most phones: Settings, then Google, then Ads, or Settings, then Privacy, then Ads). Where the law requires consent for personalised ads, our apps will ask for it before showing them, and you can change your choice later from the app’s settings.

7. Why we use data and our legal basis

We process personal data only for these purposes:

  • to answer your messages and provide support;
  • to make our apps work, fix bugs and keep them secure;
  • to deliver purchases you make and show ads in free apps, where applicable;
  • to comply with law, including tax and record-keeping duties, and to respond to lawful requests from authorities.

Under the DPDP Act, we rely on your consent, given through a clear notice, or on a legitimate use permitted by the Act (for example, when you voluntarily send us your details to get support, or when processing is needed to comply with law). You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to support@tauralune.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before it.

8. When we share data

We share personal data only:

  • with service providers that work for us (hosting, email, crash reporting, ad networks and app stores) and only for the purposes above;
  • when required by Indian law, a court order or a lawful request from a government authority; or
  • to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or Tauralune.

If Tauralune is transferred to another owner, personal data may be transferred as part of that business, under the same protections described here.

9. How long we keep data

  • Support emails: until your request is resolved, and then for up to 3 years so we can refer to earlier conversations, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: as set by the crash-reporting provider, typically 90 days.
  • Records we must keep by law (for example, tax or invoice records): for the period the law requires.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

10. Security

We follow reasonable security practices as required by the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the DPDP Act. This includes HTTPS on our website, two-factor authentication on our accounts, limited access to support mail, and encrypted connections to the services our apps use. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but if a personal data breach occurs we will notify affected users and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

11. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update your personal data;
  • ask us to erase your personal data, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw consent you have given;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so; and
  • raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied with our response, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email support@tauralune.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by replying from the email address you used with us.

12. How to request data deletion

You can ask us to delete personal data linked to you, including data from any Tauralune app account, without uninstalling the app or having an account on this website.

  1. Email support@tauralune.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include the app name and the email address or account identifier you used, if any.
  3. We confirm receipt within 3 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days. We will tell you when it is done.

We may keep a small amount of data where the law requires it (for example, transaction records needed for tax purposes). Purchase records held by Google Play or the Galaxy Store are controlled by those stores; you can manage them through your store account. Data stored only on your device is deleted when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

13. Children

Our website and apps are not directed at children unless an app’s store listing clearly says otherwise. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, as the DPDP Act requires, and we do not use children’s data for tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email support@tauralune.com and we will delete it.

14. Where data is processed

Tauralune is based in India. Some of our service providers (for example, Cloudflare, GitHub, Google and Unity) may process data on servers outside India. Such transfers happen in line with the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified by the Government of India.

15. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our website or apps change, and always before an app starts handling data in a new way. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes, we will also tell app users inside the app.
