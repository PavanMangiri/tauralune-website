Business overview

Tauralune is a sole proprietorship registered under MSME Udyam, Government of India (registration number UDYAM-AP-23-0106911). Our line of business is software and mobile application development, and software publishing.

We build consumer Android apps (everyday utilities, productivity tools and customization apps) and publish them under the Tauralune name on Google Play and the Samsung Galaxy Store. After release, we maintain each app: fixing bugs, keeping it compatible with new Android versions, and answering users by email.

We are a small studio. That means one accountable team looks after an app from the first line of code to the last support reply.

What we do

Native Android development

Apps written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and Material Design 3. We keep data on your device by default, work offline where we can, and test on a range of phones and Android versions before release.

Maintenance and user support

Bug triage, crash diagnostics, and updates for new Android releases and store requirements. Users reach us at support@tauralune.com and get a reply from the people who built the app.

Store and privacy readiness

Accurate Data safety forms, clear privacy policies, minimal permissions and a working data deletion process. We follow Google Play and Galaxy Store policies and Indian data protection law.

How we build

Language and UI
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material Design 3 with dynamic colour and dark theme.
Data and storage
Local-first storage with Room and DataStore. No account required unless a feature truly needs one.
Quality
Unit and UI tests, staged rollouts, and crash monitoring that is disclosed in each app’s privacy details.
Compatibility
We target the API level Google Play requires and update apps when new Android versions arrive.
Accessibility
TalkBack labels, scalable text, sufficient contrast and touch targets that are easy to hit.
Privacy
Only the permissions a feature needs, explained in plain language before we ask for them.

We also take on a limited number of custom Android projects for clients, under a written agreement. Write to support@tauralune.com with the subject “Business inquiry”.

Support policy

  • For an app problem, tell us the app name, app version, phone model and Android version, and what happened. Screenshots help.
  • Privacy and data requests, including deletion, are handled as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Refunds for purchases made through Google Play or the Galaxy Store follow that store’s refund policy. We will help you with the request.
  • We never ask for your passwords, OTPs or payment card details by email.
